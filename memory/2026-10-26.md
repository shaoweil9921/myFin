## Discord signal parser: strike/premium extraction (2026-10-26 evening)

- **Problem found:** many Discord option signals were being dropped because the parser never extracted `strike_price` / `premium_price`. 18 of 132 channel messages produced no signal row.
- **Root causes:**
  1. No strike/premium patterns existed in `parse_signals.py` at all — only entry/target/stop.
  2. The price regex `\$?([0-9,]+\.?[0-9]*)` could not parse the `$.55` premium format (dollar-sign then bare decimal) used by the coach.
  3. Non-trading messages (market commentary, no ticker, ticker-list newsletters) were parsed but skipped downstream.
- **Fix (in `Discord/myTrading/parse_signals.py`):** unified `_price_pat()` helper for entry/target/stop/premium/strike; new `_PRICE_NUM_RE` alternative list that handles `$68.00`, `$68`, `68.00`, `68`, and `.68`/`$.68`; `extract_strike()` / `extract_premium()`; `extract_price()` now normalizes leading-dot values to `0.xx`.
- **Result:** signal coverage went from 115/132 to **115/132 → 18→17 messages without signals**; `discord_trade_signal` now 115 rows, 114 with strike_price, 107 with premium_price, max signal_date 2026-10-26.
